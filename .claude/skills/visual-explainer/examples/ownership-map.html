Client onboarding · responsibilities

Who owns what when we take on a new client

Twelve things have to happen between "yes" and the first piece of work. Five teams touch them. Most have a clear owner. Three have none, and those are the three that went wrong last quarter.

3Activities nobody owns
  1. Telling the client who their contacts are. Everyone assumes the relationship lead does it. They assume the welcome pack does.
  2. Checking the engagement letter matches what was scoped. Risk checks the terms and the team checks the scope, but nobody compares the two.
  3. Closing the loop after the first invoice. Finance sends it, the team never hears whether it landed well.
