Where it stalls

Step 03 is most of the wait, and most of that is waiting for the supplier. The security questionnaire goes out, and nobody chases it. Legal's own review takes two or three days once the answers are in.

Sending the questionnaire at step 01, whenever the requester ticks "client data", would start that clock about a week earlier.
